The problem

Developer events have no affordable way to keep attendees engaged once the event ends.

What events use today
Printed badges and swag

Worn or handed out for two days, then thrown away or put in a drawer.

The spend stops working when the event ends.
Event apps

Built around the schedule. Once the event is over, there’s nothing to come back to.

Engagement ends with the last session.
