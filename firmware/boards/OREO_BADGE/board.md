The Oreo Badge is an open-hardware conference badge built around an
ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8 module (16 MB flash, 8 MB octal PSRAM), with a 2.0"
320x240 ST7789 display, a TSOP38238 infrared receiver, and USB-C (native USB
plus a CP2102N debug UART). It runs OreoOS, an open-source
MicroPython-based operating system with an app SDK, OTA updates and the RV565
video pipeline.

Hardware (KiCad), OreoOS and documentation: https://oreo.elixpo.com
