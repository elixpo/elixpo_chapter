The research · arXiv preprint
arXiv:2610.35935 · cs.AR

RV565: Cross-Layer Co-Design for Frame-Independent Video Playback on MicroPython-Class ESP32-S3 Systems

Ayushman Bhattacharya

  • Frames stored in the display's own pixel format: nothing to convert
  • Frame-independent decoding, on both cores, with DMA to the display
  • 24 frames a second on an ESP32-S3 SPI display
  • Now in Arduino, the Espressif registry and PyPI; featured on the Adafruit blog
